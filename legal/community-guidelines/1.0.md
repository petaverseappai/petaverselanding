# Community Guidelines

These Community Guidelines apply to all content and conduct on PetaVerse’s social and community features, including PawHub, communities, posts, comments, Lost & Found reports, adoption listings, and provider interactions. They supplement our Terms & Conditions.

Our goal is a safe, respectful community for pet owners. Content or behavior that breaks these guidelines may be removed, and accounts may be restricted, suspended, or terminated.

## 1. Be Respectful

Treat other members with respect. Do not harass, threaten, bully, or abuse others, and do not post hateful or discriminatory content.

## 2. Keep It Honest

Do not impersonate others or post false, misleading, or fraudulent information. This is especially important for Lost & Found posts, adoption listings, and service-provider listings, where inaccurate information can cause real harm.

## 3. Respect Privacy and Rights

Do not share other people’s personal information without their consent. Only post content you have the right to share, and respect copyright and other intellectual-property rights.

## 4. No Harmful or Illegal Content

Do not post illegal content, spam, malware, or content that promotes cruelty to animals or other unlawful activity. Content depicting animal abuse is prohibited.

## 5. Animal Welfare

Adoption and rehoming features exist to help animals find good homes. Do not use them for the illegal sale of animals or any activity that endangers animal welfare.

## 6. Reporting and Enforcement

If you see content that violates these guidelines, please report it or contact us at support@petaverseapp.com. We review reports and may remove content, restrict features, or suspend or terminate accounts. We may also cooperate with lawful authorities where appropriate.
