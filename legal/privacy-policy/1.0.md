# Privacy Policy

PetaVerse (“PetaVerse”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is operated by [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME], an individual based in Lebanon. PetaVerse is intended for users aged 16 and over. If you are under 16, please do not use the Service. See the “Children and Minimum Age” section below.

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use the Service:

| Category | Examples | Stored |
|---|---|---|
| Account information | Phone number, email address, name, hashed password, date of birth (optional), profile photo, account identifier | Yes |
| Pet information | Pet name, species, breed, date of birth, gender, size, coat/pelage, microchip number and location, sterilization status, vaccinations, medications, weight, activity, appointments, medical and other documents, health notes, and pet photos | Yes |

We do not currently collect payment information, because PetaVerse does not currently offer paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. If this changes, we will update this Policy before enabling payments.

## 3. Phone Number and OTP Authentication

We collect and process your phone number to create and authenticate your account and to send verification codes (one-time passwords) by SMS. Verification codes are delivered through our SMS provider, Twilio.

We do store your phone number as part of your account. We apply rate-limiting and abuse-detection measures to OTP requests; for this purpose we process technical signals such as IP address transiently and only in hashed form, and we do not store raw IP addresses.

## 4. Location Data

With your permission, PetaVerse may access your device’s location to provide location-based features, such as nearby pet service providers, Lost & Found functionality, setting your profile location, and tracking a pet’s walk (distance and speed).

The Service may request precise (GPS) location. For most features, location is accessed only while you are using that feature (for example, when you drop a Lost & Found pin or set your profile location).

During an active walk-tracking session, the app may continue to access your location while the app is running in the background (minimized) in order to measure the walk. Walk tracking stops when you end the session or close the app, and you can use a timer-only mode without location if you prefer. PetaVerse does not continuously track your location when you are not using a location-based feature.

Where you choose to share a location (for example, in a Lost & Found report or an adoption listing), that location may be visible to other users as part of that feature.

## 5. Photos and Uploaded Files

When you upload photos, documents, or other files — such as pet photos, profile photos, medical documents, Lost & Found photos, adoption photos, community uploads, or provider images — that content is stored using a third-party cloud storage provider (Cloudflare R2).

We do not need to expose our detailed infrastructure, but you should understand that your uploaded content is processed and stored by service providers on our behalf.

## 6. AI Pet Assistant

PetaVerse offers an AI assistant (chat). When you use it, the messages, pet health context, and any images you choose to attach are sent to our AI provider, Google (Gemini), which processes them to generate a response. Your conversations are also stored in your account so you can revisit them.

Please do not submit information you do not wish to be processed by an AI provider. AI-generated information is provided for general informational and educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care. AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate for a particular animal.

“Pet Vision” is a separate feature that simulates how your pet may perceive colors. It applies a visual filter to your camera image entirely on your device; the image is not uploaded to us or to any AI provider for this feature. An image only leaves your device if you choose to save it to your gallery or share it in a post.

## 7. Push Notifications and Reminders

If you enable notifications, we register a push notification token for your device through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) so we can send you reminders (for example, feeding, medication, vaccinations, grooming, and appointments) and other Service notifications. We store the device token, platform, and a device identifier for this purpose. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

## 8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The mobile app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the Service is used and Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose crashes so we can improve stability. These tools collect app usage events, crash diagnostics, and related device and app information.

Our websites use Cloudflare analytics for basic, privacy-respecting traffic measurement.

## 9. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

- Creating, authenticating, and managing your account
- Managing your pets and storing their health records
- Providing reminders for feeding, medication, vaccinations, grooming and appointments
- Providing Lost & Found, adoption, communities (PawHub), and service-provider discovery features
- Providing the AI assistant and on-device features such as Pet Vision
- Sending notifications you have enabled
- Providing customer support
- Detecting, preventing, and investigating abuse, fraud, and spam, and securing the Service
- Maintaining and improving the Service
- Complying with legal obligations

We do not use your information for purposes that are incompatible with those described above.

## 11. International Data Transfers

PetaVerse is operated from Lebanon, but several of our service providers (such as Twilio, Cloudflare, Google/Firebase, and our email provider) operate internationally. As a result, some of your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service.

If you delete your account, we delete your personal information and your pets’ records from our active systems, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain certain information (for example, for security, fraud-prevention, legal, or accounting purposes). Residual copies may persist temporarily in routine backups and are removed in the normal course of our backup cycles.

If you create an account but do not verify your phone number, we automatically delete that unverified account after approximately 30 days.

## 13. Your Privacy Rights

You can:

- Access and update most of your information within the app
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile Tab → delete account), which removes your account and associated pet records as described above
- Withdraw permissions (such as location, camera, photos, or notifications) through your device settings

The exact rights available to you depend on your jurisdiction. If you are located in the European Economic Area or another region with specific data-protection laws, you may have additional rights (such as objection, restriction, or portability); contact us and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

## 14. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include encryption in transit (TLS), hashed password storage, authentication and access controls, rate limiting, logging and monitoring, secure cloud storage, and restricted administrative access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 15. Children and Minimum Age

The Service is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@petaverseapp.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
